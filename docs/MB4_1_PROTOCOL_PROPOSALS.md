# MB4.1 — outcome-blind protocol proposals

Status: `PROPOSED_AWAITING_INDEPENDENT_HUMAN_APPROVAL`.

These proposals make the three unresolved method meanings reviewable without
pretending that an automated implementation can approve a scientific protocol.
Their exact machine-readable content and digests are in
`artifacts/v5-final/method-native/mb4-1-protocol-drafts-v1.json`.

## V5 no-rebuild

The proposal freezes the source structural candidate whitelist and source
order. At each accepted child it builds the normal current-runtime catalog,
retains only whitelisted structural IDs, and recomputes numerical constraints,
predictions, curvature coordinates, and resources. New structural candidates
are barred. An unavailable or semantically incompatible whitelisted candidate
is recorded as `STALE_UNAVAILABLE` and is not executed. Thus the causal
contrast is structural replenishment, not stale numerical state.

## Magnitude control

The proposal classifies this comparator as a prospectively registered control,
not as a parent-native algorithm. It uses the pinned squared-residual magnitude,
removes one lowest-score physical generator at a time, breaks ties by structural
ID, reoptimizes and fully recounts after each committed deletion, and then
recomputes all surviving scores. Rejection rolls back the entire transaction.
Stopping and work-cap behavior are explicit in the artifact.

## V4.1 H2/H4 sentinel

The proposal requires a new H2/H4-only, outcome-blind sentinel freeze separate
from the 90-item development queue. It freezes source, ordered candidates,
equivalence classes, work, optimizer, acceptance, RNG, executor, and environment
before candidate outcomes. FCI, candidate energies, historical outcome ranks,
and development results are forbidden screening inputs. Historical sentinels
remain provenance only and cannot be copied.

## Authorization boundary

The approval record is intentionally empty. No executor, H2/H4 queue, candidate
energy, development execution, or performance claim is authorized. Independent
human approval must name the exact protocol digests in a new immutable approval
artifact; it must not edit or replace this proposal artifact.
