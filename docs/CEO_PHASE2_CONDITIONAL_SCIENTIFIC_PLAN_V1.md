# CEO Post-Growth Compression — Conditional Phase 2 Scientific Plan v1

## Validation First; Ranking Only When Phase 1 Proves It Is Needed

**Status:** conditional plan; not authorized for execution

**Entry authority:** terminal Phase-1 decision artifact

**Historical Phase-1 E3 outcomes:** development evidence

**Primary purpose:** independent validation of a Phase-1 signal and, only when
necessary, development of an efficient acquisition method

## 0. Phase-2 entry rule

Phase 2 is not automatically entered when Phase 1 finishes.

| Phase-1 terminal state | Phase-2 authorization |
|---|---|
| no joint resource signal | none; close the semantic-joint route |
| structural targets but no accuracy-feasible singleton gap | none; bounded negative result |
| gap in exactly one molecule family | targeted replication only |
| gap in at least two families but no optimized-prefix advantage | structural analysis may continue; deployment-superiority claim prohibited |
| gap and prefix advantage in at least two families | structural validation authorized |
| previous row and bounded enumeration exceeds prospective work cap | ranking-method development also authorized |

Phase 2 may not change a Phase-1 outcome, target identity, grammar, optimizer,
or comparison rule and then describe the new result as a continuation of the
same confirmatory study.

## 1. Branch A — targeted replication

Enter when Phase 1 finds a joint accuracy-feasible frontier gap in only one
molecule family.

### Question

Does the same frozen target language and optimization contract reproduce the
direction of the gap in an outcome-blind selected second family or new
geometry?

### Rules

- no ranking method;
- no descriptor fitting;
- no grammar or threshold change;
- one new source family is preferred to another geometry of the positive
  family;
- all outcomes remain limited replication evidence.

If replication fails, stop. Do not add a new transformation family from the
failure.

## 2. Branch B — structural validation

Enter when Phase 1 finds both singleton-frontier and prefix-frontier value in
at least two molecule families.

### 2.1 Primary confirmatory hypothesis

Under the unchanged Phase-1 protocol,

\[
\widehat C_{\mathrm{joint,obs}}^*(\varepsilon)
<
\widehat C_{\mathrm{singleton,obs}}^*(\varepsilon)
\]

and the joint point remains below the fully reoptimized prefix frontier in
untouched E4 evidence.

### 2.2 E4 design

Before the first Phase-1 E3 candidate energy, freeze either:

- two out-of-development molecule families; or
- one new family plus a second independently selected family/geometry if
  source-generation cost makes two new families infeasible.

Selection is based on predefined feasibility and coverage rules:

- exact-statevector qubit/memory feasibility;
- availability of a paper-era-compatible CEO* source protocol;
- coverage of at least two distinct correlation/CEO-block-structure regimes;
- no use of expected compressibility or preliminary candidate energy.

The molecule family is the statistical clustering unit.

### 2.3 Mandatory robustness

- same canonical paper-era resource definition;
- one fixed generic compiler configuration applied symmetrically to source,
  singleton, joint, and prefix circuits;
- source and target unitary/state checks at their registered evidence level;
- case-level failure and raw-work flow.

If the canonical advantage disappears after generic compilation, retain the
canonical result but restrict its claim to the registered CEO representation.

## 3. Branch C — conditional ranking-method development

This branch requires both:

1. Phase-1 structural and prefix value; and
2. complete bounded enumeration exceeding the frozen prospective work cap in
   at least two positive E3 sources.

If enumeration is affordable, ranking is not a contribution and this branch is
`STOP_RANKING_NOT_NEEDED`.

### 3.1 Reference and baselines

- **B4:** complete bounded-enumeration observed reference within the frozen
  candidate universe;
- **B3e:** generic joint constrained-second-order ranking using the same
  Hessian/gradient information as B5;
- **B5:** CEO-aware joint ranking with at most two structural descriptors.

B3e and B5 must share:

- CandidateUniverseID;
- candidate materialization and optimizer;
- source, gradient, and second-order information;
- work cap and terminal certificate;
- tie-breaking and failure semantics.

### 3.2 Development and lock

E3 is explicitly a method-development dataset. After Phase-1 E3 results:

1. select one mechanism hypothesis;
2. define at most two CEO descriptors;
3. freeze B5 score, missing policy, tie-break, and work cap in a new Ranking
   Lock;
4. do not inspect E4 candidate outcomes before the lock;
5. run B3e and B5 once on E4.

This is valid method development, not post-hoc confirmation, because E4 remains
untouched and B5 is never revised against it.

### 3.3 Ranking endpoint

Let \(G_{B4}\) be the verified CNOT gain recovered by bounded enumeration and
\(G_m(W)\) the gain recovered by method \(m\) under work cap \(W\).

Report

\[
\rho_m(W)=\frac{G_m(W)}{G_{B4}}
\]

only when \(G_{B4}>0\). Otherwise the recovery fraction is `NOT_APPLICABLE`.

Ranking-method value requires on E4:

- B5 recovers strictly more verified gain than B3e under the same cap;
- B5 uses less registered work than B4;
- accepted endpoints pass the unchanged scientific certificates;
- the effect is not confined to one geometry of one family.

## 4. Optional analyses after the primary decision

These do not block Phase-2 primary completion:

- lifecycle maps for accepted/retained blocks;
- direct compressed-topology replay;
- TETRIS subset diagnostics;
- Co-ADAPT-source secondary application;
- expanded generic compiler/topology robustness;
- source-state-rewrite noise sensitivity;
- deployment break-even calculation.

Each optional analysis needs a named question and a new artifact namespace. It
must not change the primary target language, B5 lock, or E4 result.

## 5. Measurement and work in Phase 2

Raw work counters remain mandatory. Paper Measurement Cost is optional.

It becomes a Phase-2 deliverable only if all of the following hold:

- the exact CEO-paper conversion is reconstructed;
- operation coverage is explicit;
- compression work can be expressed without mixing unlike units;
- missing conversions remain `null` rather than estimated from CNOT savings.

No shot or Measurement Cost claim is required for the structural or ranking
route. CNOT reduction never implies measurement reduction by itself.

## 6. Phase-2 scientific stages

### SCI-P2-S0 — terminal Phase-1 import

- verify the terminal Phase-1 tag and all source/candidate/result digests;
- import only permitted summaries and frozen code;
- select the authorized Phase-2 branch from the decision table.

### SCI-P2-S1 — confirmation and method lock

- finalize E4 identities/source generation before opening outcomes;
- freeze Branch A/B protocol unchanged from Phase 1;
- if Branch C is authorized, create the post-E3 Ranking Lock before E4.

### SCI-P2-S2 — source generation and parity

- generate E4 CEO* sources with the frozen paper-era-compatible protocol;
- prohibit FCI-guided source stopping;
- construct canonical B2 and source eligibility evidence.

### SCI-P2-S3 — confirmatory execution

- execute all registered singleton, bounded joint, and conditional prefix rows;
- execute B3e/B5 only when Branch C is authorized;
- preserve all terminal statuses and registered work.

### SCI-P2-S4 — locked analysis

- join reporting-only references after completion;
- aggregate by molecule-family cluster;
- report canonical and fixed-compiler robustness separately;
- apply the route-specific decision criteria without changing the method.

### SCI-P2-S5 — release and manuscript decision

- reproduce code-to-equation and result-to-artifact links;
- release negative, null, failed, and positive rows;
- select a manuscript route based on evidence, not target venue preference.

## 7. Publication routes

### Structural-method route

Requires:

- Phase-1 singleton and prefix value;
- a CEO-native analytical or bounded-certified structural rule;
- untouched E4 transfer at the declared level;
- symmetric compiler robustness;
- complete failure and work reporting.

### Ranking-method route

Requires all structural-method conditions plus:

- ranking necessity;
- identical B3e/B5 information and candidate universe;
- B5 advantage over B3e on untouched E4;
- materially lower work than B4.

### Negative-result route

If a sound Phase-1 signal fails E4, release a bounded non-transfer result only
when the candidate universe, failure flow, and limitations provide useful
evidence. Do not manufacture novelty with additional unrelated mechanisms.

## 8. Phase-2 prohibited changes

- replacing failed E4 cases;
- changing grammar after E4 structure or outcomes are known;
- relaxing energy/stationarity thresholds;
- adding a B5 descriptor after E4 failure;
- increasing only B5's work cap;
- calling new geometries independent molecule families;
- hiding rejected/cap-rejected rows;
- converting engineering parallelism into lower scientific work;
- promoting an optional analysis to the primary claim after seeing it succeed.

## 9. Phase-2 Definition of Done

Phase 2 is complete when the authorized branch—not every possible branch—has:

1. an immutable Phase-1 parent;
2. a pre-outcome E4 or replication lock;
3. terminal flow for every authorized row;
4. unchanged source, optimizer, acceptance, and resource semantics;
5. a molecule-family-aware analysis;
6. a route-specific positive or negative decision;
7. a clean-clone reproducible package;
8. no unauthorized optional analysis in the primary conclusion.
