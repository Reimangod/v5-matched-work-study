# Claim boundary

This repository studies post-processing compression of fixed CEO* checkpoints.
It does not modify CEO* growth and does not establish end-to-end cost
superiority over CEO*, ordinary ADAPT, or any external method.

Historical V4.1/V5/V5.1 and PRA-path artifacts are immutable development
evidence. Best-found results are not called matched-work results. FCI or exact
reference energy is offline reporting data only and may not affect candidate
selection, budgets, stopping, winner selection, reruns, or queue construction.

Parameter removal is not a physical-resource reduction unless the generator or
block is physically removed and the full circuit is recounted. Exact noiseless
statevector evidence does not support hardware/noise claims. H2O and N2, if
later authorized, are validation cases of this study and are not relabelled PRA
prospective evidence.
